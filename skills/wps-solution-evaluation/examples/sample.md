# Sample: WPS Solution Evaluation

```markdown
# WPS Solution Evaluation

## 1. Decision Context
- Problem: team leads cannot detect project risk early enough
- Edition: Enterprise
- User role: team lead
- Scenario: ongoing execution
- Decision needed: how to improve progress visibility

## 2. Options
### Option A
- Summary: add a richer dashboard
- Assumptions: users will proactively open it
- Scope boundary: status viewing only

### Option B
- Summary: proactive risk summary inside the project view
- Assumptions: users prefer in-context signals to separate dashboards
- Scope boundary: focuses on active projects only

## 3. Comparison Table
| Option | User Value | Strategic Fit | Complexity | Collaboration Cost | Risk | Validation Path | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Medium | Medium | Medium | Low | Medium | Medium | May become a passive report page |
| B | High | High | Medium | Low | Medium | High | Better tied to action-taking |

## 5. Recommendation
- Recommended option: Option B
- Why it wins: better supports the actual team lead job without adding a detached reporting workflow
```
